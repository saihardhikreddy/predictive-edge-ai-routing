          Set up each phone
          
          
            
            
          1. Install the app. Open the latest release on the phone, download MeshTestbed.apk, allow installs from your browser, and install. To update, install over the old app. Uninstalling first erases your saved name.
            2. 
            
          2. Check the build. Every phone should run the same one. This phone runs the browser demo.
            3. 
            
          3. Give it a name. Friends find you by this name, even when you are out of their range. Tap your name at the top to change it.
            4. 
            
          4. Press Start and allow Nearby devices. On Android 11 or older, also switch Location on, because Android needs it to scan for Bluetooth.
            5. 
            
          5. Keep the screen on. The app holds it awake. Android slows Bluetooth scanning for apps in the background.

          Reading the signal
          
          
          Bluetooth signal is shown in dBm. It is always negative, and closer to zero means stronger.
          
          
          
          Words on screen follow the same scale: Strong is −60 or better, Good to −72, Fair to −82, and Weak below that.
          
        
          

        
          
          
          Test plan
          
          
          Run these in order with three to five phones. Tick each one off as it passes; the ticks stay on this phone.
          
          
          0 of 10 done
          
          
            
            
          1. 
              
            Same room, one hop
            
              
            3 phones within a few metres, nothing blocked, Range limit off
            
              
            Do
            On phone A, open Send, pick C under Nearby and send a message.
            
              
            Pass when
            A shows Delivered in 1 hop and C shows the message from A by name.
            
              
            
            2. 
            
          2. 
              
            Force a relay
            
              
            The same 3 phones
            
              
            Do
            On A, tap C in Mesh and choose Block this phone. On C, block A. Send from A to C again.
            
              
            Pass when
            The light hops through B on the map and A shows Delivered in 2 hops. With 4 phones in a chain A–B–C–D, A to D takes 3 hops.
            
              
            
            3. 
            
          3. 
              
            Reach a phone you cannot hear
            
              
            The chain from the previous test
            
              
            Do
            Wait about 30 seconds, then open Send on A.
            
              
            Pass when
            C is listed by name under Through the mesh with 2 hops, and a message sent to it is delivered.
            
              
            
            4. 
            
          4. 
              
            Store, carry, forward
            
              
            The chain, Learned route on every phone
            
              
            Do
            Carry B into another room so A and C are cut off. Send from A to C. After a minute, bring B back.
            
              
            Pass when
            The message waits as On its way while B is away, then is delivered once B returns. Try the same in Flooding to compare.
            
              
            
            5. 
            
          5. 
              
            Catch an attacker
            
              
            4 phones: A and C, with two relays B and D between them
            
              
            Do
            On B, open Defend and switch on Act as the attacker. From A, send 15 to 20 messages to C, or run a Lab experiment.
            
              
            Pass when
            B's suspicion bar on A climbs, A announces that B was cut out of its routes, and later messages reach C through D. Afterwards, switch the attacker off and tap Forgive all phones.

          6. 
              
            Learned route against flooding
            
              
            4 to 5 phones in a chain
            
              
            Do
            Tap Reset results on every phone. Set Learned route on every phone, then in Lab run 30 messages, one every 3 s. Repeat with Flooding on every phone.
            
              
            Pass when
            Both rows of the results table are filled. Learned routing should send fewer frames for each delivered message.

          7. 
              
            Count the whole mesh
            
              
            The chain of 4 to 5 phones
            
              
            Do
            Open Mesh on the phone at one end of the chain and wait about a minute.
            
              
            Pass when
            It hears only one phone directly but says the mesh has all of them. Send an SOS from the far end: the SOS count goes up on every phone, and it shows as answered once it arrives.

          8. 
              
            Check the forecast
            
              
            Any layout, and the store-carry-forward walk
            
              
            Do
            In Send, pick a phone and read the forecast before sending. Do it in the same room, through the chain, and while the relay is away.
            
              
            Pass when
            Each receipt shows the forecast next to the real time, and Lab reports how many forecasts held. Walk the relay away and back a few times first, so the phone learns how long it usually stays away.

            Reach further with Long Range
            
              
            2 phones that show Long Range as supported in Route
            
              
            Do
            Walk the phones apart, in a corridor or outdoors, until one disappears from Mesh with Long Range off. Switch Long Range on in Route on both phones and wait 15 seconds.
            
              
            Pass when
            The phone comes back with a Long Range badge and a message to it is delivered in 1 hop. Note both distances for the report.

          Show it in five minutes
          
          
          A running order for a lecturer or a viva, with three phones on the desk.
          
          
            
            
          1. Mesh. Your phone is the amber lantern. The others stand where their signal puts them.
            2. 
            
          2. Send in the same room. One hop, straight across.
            3. 
            
          3. Block the far phone and send again. The light visibly hops through the middle phone, and the receipt says 2 hops.
            4. 
            
          4. Route. Show what the phone has learned from each hop, and who it expects to meet next.
            5. 
            
          5. Census. Back on Mesh, point out that the phone counts everyone in the mesh, including phones it cannot hear.
            6. 
            
          6. Forecast. In Send, read the forecast aloud, send, and compare it with the receipt.
            7. 
            
          7. Defend. Make one phone the attacker and let the others cut it out.
            8. 
            
          8. Lab. Finish on the table comparing learned routing with flooding.

          If something goes wrong
          
          
          Phones do not see each other
          Press Start on both, check Bluetooth is on and Nearby devices is allowed, keep both screens on, and give it ten seconds. On Android 11 or older, Location must be on.
          
          
          A far phone is missing from Through the mesh
          Names travel across the mesh every 30 seconds, and only along phones that are running the app. Check that every phone in the chain shows Running.
          
          
          The app asks for my name again
          Check the build number at the bottom of Lab. If it is old, install the latest APK over the app without uninstalling it.
          
          
          Phones in one room take two hops
          Check that no phone is blocked and that Range limit in Route is set to Off.
          
          
          Messages arrive, but slowly
          Each hop opens a Bluetooth connection, which takes about 0.3 to 1.5 seconds. That is real, and worth reporting.
          
        
          

        
          
          
          Automated tests
          
          
          Every build is checked before it is published. 35 scenarios run the real routing engine on virtual phones over a simulated Bluetooth radio: same-room delivery, chains of up to four phones, store-carry-forward, names reaching far phones, the census counting a whole chain and its SOS calls, forecasts checked against real delivery times, Bluetooth 5 Long Range joining phones that normal Bluetooth cannot reach, and an attacker being caught. The finished APK is then installed on an Android emulator, which checks the name screen, a restart, every chapter, and the page for crashes.
          
          
          Results for each build are on the project's Actions page on GitHub.
