🧠 How the Cognitive Edge Mesh Works
🌊 1. The Problem: Legacy Flooding ("The Shouting Room")
In BitChat-style meshes every phone that hears a message rebroadcasts it once, up to 7 hops. One broadcast is one transmission, but every neighbour pays to receive it, and neighbours that rebroadcast in the same time slot collide. The "Network Collisions" card counts those collisions live.
🧠 2. The Solution: Predictive MARL Routing ("The Smart Courier")
Each phone learns its own small Q-function from only what it can see locally: neighbour beacons, signal strength, and how likely each neighbour is to meet the destination soon (learned from encounter history). It either hands the message to one neighbour or carries it until a better relay comes into range. When two phones meet they average their model weights (gossip federated learning). No locations or messages are shared.
🚨 3. Cognitive Triage & 4. Trust IDS
An on-device triage model spots emergencies and encodes them as a few semantic tokens (about 110 B → 30 B on air). SOS messages get priority, two copies and no exploration. Every hop is confirmed by a two-hop ACK; a node that keeps swallowing packets drives up a CUSUM change detector compared with its neighbourhood, and it is quarantined automatically (scenario 4).
